## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Visible guard interventions
Every move the guard stops SHALL make the board and the guard's row in "Who decided" glow briefly in the guard's color, visible at any speed and shortened when the visitor prefers reduced motion, and the row SHALL count the moves the guard stopped in the current run.

#### Scenario: Guard stops a move
- **WHEN** the guard rejects System One's proposal
- **THEN** the board and the guard's row glow, and the count of stopped moves increases by one
