# Design

- **One repository.** The experiments share the from-scratch MLP, calibration, statistics, the evaluation
  protocol and the 3D views, so they stay in one repository named `lab`. "System One" remains the name of
  experiment 01 and of its URL (`/systemone/`), so shared links keep working.
- **Write-ups.** The text of the former README moves into `docs/` without rewording. Citations in
  `docs/systemone.md` keep their numbers; `docs/handwriting.md` renumbers its own.
- **Folder rename.** After the quadruped study: `mv systemoneweb lab`. The notes kept for this project
  (preferences, research standards) move with it, because they are stored per folder path.
