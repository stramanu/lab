Inside System One

The live forward pass behind the current decision. Nodes light up with their real activations, and only the connections that contribute most to this decision are drawn (positive, negative). Drag to orbit. The view re-runs the forward pass for display; that work is not counted in the cost.

Everything runs in your browser: the environments, the planners, the network and its backprop, written from scratch in TypeScript. Cost is counted in compute units: nodes expanded or physics steps simulated by the planner or the guard, and one unit per network forward pass. Method, protocol, results and references are in the repository README.
